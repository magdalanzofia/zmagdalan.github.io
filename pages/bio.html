About

Zofia Magdalan is an emerging fashion designer and visual artist. She studied graphic design at an art high school before graduating in fashion design. Her creative interests span fashion and visual arts, with a particular focus on graphic design, painting and collage. Her practice is driven by a search for her own creative voice, both in fashion and in her artistic work. Upcycling plays an important role in her approach to fashion design, offering a way to give existing materials new meaning and extend their life. She is particularly interested in the potential of objects and textiles and the possibilities they offer when reimagined as garments. Her graduate collection received distinctions from NUMÉRO, WHITEMAD and Risk Made in Warsaw. She continues to develop her work across fashion and visual arts, exploring different ways of expressing ideas through materials, images and form.
